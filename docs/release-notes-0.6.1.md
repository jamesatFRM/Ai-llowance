# Ai-llowance 0.6.1 — Your dashboard. Your way.

Build 246 adds account-by-account comparisons, a flexible dashboard, glass dropdown lists and clearer notification recovery.

- **Your charts, your comparisons.** Each account has its own direct chart selector and Off, WoW, MoM or YoY comparison. Compare seven completed UTC days of observed consumption against the matching earlier period, with a short summary of the change. Missing or uneven coverage is explained instead of turned into a misleading percentage. Subscription allowance points and API dollars remain separate.
- **A dashboard that adapts.** Collapse account cards, the usage overview or the activity heatmap individually, or use Collapse all. Tiles move into available space with smooth animations that respect Reduce Motion. Narrow heatmaps switch to readable four-hour blocks. Account chart, comparison and collapse choices are saved.
- **Glass menus that match the app.** Opened chart, comparison, filter and account menus now share rounded glass panels, contextual icons, colored highlights and selected checkmarks. Flat lists support keyboard navigation, type-ahead and Escape. Settings keeps native Mac controls.
- **Clear notification recovery.** See when notifications are disabled in macOS, open Notifications Settings and return to recheck access. Already-authorized access and interrupted permission replies are handled correctly. A late permission reply cannot turn alerts back on after you switch them off. A test notification action helps check delivery; macOS permissions and Focus still control presentation.
- **400 days of daily history.** Daily consumption summaries retain 400 days to support prior-year comparisons. Detailed history remains bounded to seven days and 4,096 samples. Updates preserve the existing storage location and saved account settings. Retention cannot recover readings that were never collected or already expired.

Signed and notarized for Apple silicon and Intel Macs on macOS 14 or later. In Ai-llowance, open Settings → Updates → Check for Updates, then Install and Relaunch.

The release artwork uses illustrative UI, not an actual app screenshot. Comparison availability depends on recorded history; real notification delivery still depends on the Mac's permission and Focus settings. No new background polling or timers were added.
